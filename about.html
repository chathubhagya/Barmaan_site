About Us

Based in Erbil, Barmaan Trading Company (BTC) is a premier industrial partner dedicated to elevating operational efficiency and safety standards across Iraq and the Kurdistan Region, based on world-class standards. As we respond to industry needs and provide after-sales and customer service, we are not just a supplier; we are a technical and consulting service center. Bridging the gap between high-end international manufacturers and local industrial needs, BTC offers a synergistic approach that combines strategic procurement with advanced design, implementation & construction, commissioning, calibration, inspection, repair, and testing services. Our commitment to QHSE (Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment) and adherence to international standards (API, ASME, ISO) ensures that we deliver reliable, precise, and safe solutions to the Oil & Gas, Energy, and Process industries.
