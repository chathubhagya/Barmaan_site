BARMAAN TRADING COMPANY (BTC)

Based in Erbil, Barmaan Trading Company (BTC) is a premier industrial partner dedicated to elevating operational efficiency and safety standards across Iraq and the Kurdistan Region, based on world-class standards. As we respond to industry needs and provide after-sales and customer service, we are not just a supplier; we are a technical and consulting service center. Bridging the gap between high-end international manufacturers and local industrial needs, BTC offers a synergistic approach that combines strategic procurement with advanced design, implementation & construction, commissioning, calibration, inspection, repair, and testing services. Our commitment to QHSE (Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment) and adherence to international standards (API, ASME, ISO) ensures that we deliver reliable, precise, and safe solutions to the Oil & Gas, Energy, and Process industries.

QHSE & Technical Compliance

Our commitment to QHSE (Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment) and adherence to international standards such as API, ASME, and ISO ensures that BTC delivers reliable, precise and safe industrial solutions.

  • Traceability: All calibration certificates traceable to NIST / UKAS standards.
  • Safety Protocols: Technicians trained in H2S Awareness, Confined Space Entry, and Advanced First Aid.
  • Compliance: Fully compliant with Iraqi and KRG industrial regulations.
  • Industries Served: Oil & Gas, Energy Sector, Process Industries, Manufacturing.

Have questions about Barmaan Trading Company (BTC)?

Find answers to the most common questions about BTC, your trusted industrial partner in Iraq and the Kurdistan Region.

What is Barmaan Trading Company (BTC)?

BTC is a premier industrial partner based in Erbil, providing precision engineering, industrial procurement, calibration, inspection, and testing services. We bridge international manufacturers and local industrial needs with a focus on operational efficiency, safety, and world-class standards.

What industries does BTC serve?

BTC serves the Oil & Gas, Energy, and Process industries, delivering reliable, precise, and safe solutions through adherence to international standards (API, ASME, ISO) and a strong commitment to QHSE (Quality, Health, Safety, and Environment).

What services does BTC provide?

BTC offers a wide range of services including:

  • Engineering & Project Services: Design, Construction, Commissioning
  • Calibration, Inspection & Testing: Pressure, Temperature, Dimensional, Mass & Volume, Electrical, Torque & Force, Storage Tank & Flowmeter Calibration, Fire & Gas System Calibration
  • Inspection & NDT: PT, MT, MPI, UT, Visual Inspection
  • Functional Testing & Certification: Lifting equipment, Hydrotesting, On-site support
  • Trading & Industrial Supply: Process instrumentation, High-pressure components, Well service unit spares, Safety & Measurement equipment

How does BTC ensure quality and compliance?

All certificates are fully traceable to NIST/UKAS standards. Technicians are trained in H2S awareness, Confined Space Entry, and Advanced First Aid. BTC operations are fully compliant with Iraqi and KRG industrial regulations.

What makes BTC different from other industrial suppliers?

BTC offers the Barmaan Advantage:

  • Technical Expertise: We calibrate, verify, and repair, not just sell.
  • Consultancy: Expert consulting for industrial projects.
  • Rapid Mobilization: Strategically located in Erbil for fast deployment.

Where is BTC located?

BTC headquarters are based in Erbil, Iraq, with strategically located teams to ensure fast service and deployment for all industrial projects.
